JOB TITLE: Junior Data Analyst

ROLE SUMMARY
We are looking for a junior data analyst to prepare, clean, analyse, and communicate data that supports business decisions.

REQUIRED SKILLS
Python, SQL, Microsoft Excel, data cleaning, data visualization, and statistics.

PREFERRED SKILLS
Power BI, Pandas, NumPy, and Tableau are preferred. Experience with Git is a plus.

EXPERIENCE REQUIREMENT
At least 1 year of relevant work experience is required. Candidates may also demonstrate relevant experience through internships or substantial projects, which should be reviewed by a human recruiter.

EDUCATION REQUIREMENT
A bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Data Science, or a related field is preferred.

RESPONSIBILITIES
Prepare datasets, validate data quality, write queries, analyse trends, create visual reports, and explain findings clearly to non-technical stakeholders.
